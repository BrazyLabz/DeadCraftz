How to Survive

Daylight is for scavenging. A few slow zombies roam. At night the horde comes out — if they see light they rush you and tear down anything in their way. Hole up in a building, board up the doors and windows with planks, and stay dark.

Touch: left thumb = move (push all the way to sprint) · drag right side = look · tap = place / use · hold = mine / attack · buttons for jump, hit/fire, use/aim/eat, sneak (exit car), bag, torch, drop.
